Web Books Take a Deeper Dive The Crop Protection Network Web Books series are multi-chapter publications that take a deeper dive into specific crop diseases, weeds, insects, and other pest management hot topics. Each web book chapter is authored by a team of researchers and extension specialists and is peer-reviewed by subject-matter experts.
